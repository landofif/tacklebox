# Catches and rarity

- **Catches** (2026-10-30, `display/CatchWatch`; it used foecollab's catch hook before, now the same for everyone): `InGameHudOverlayMixin.setTitle` passes the title; one glyph in U+E000-E999 is the caught fish's icon, so the ids of your fish in the inventory are noted. For 3 seconds after, each tick looks for a fish stack (custom_data with `fish`) whose `catcher` is you and whose `id` was not there. That fish goes to `CommunityGoal.onCatch`, `Compendium.onCatch` (species, variant, size, location ids as in the NBT) and `EventHud.onCatch`, and its item (custom_data and name) to the catch title (`CatchTitle.onFish`, `huds/catch-title.md`). Research section 3.
  - The catch effects (2026-11-17, `catch-effects.md`): the fish it finds also starts the catch ring and the big catch moment (`effects`, once per catch title). With a full inventory, 500 ms after the title (`fallback`), the server's subtitle stands in when it came within 1 s of the title, before or after (`onSubtitle`, from `InGameHudOverlayMixin.setSubtitle`): rarity from its badge, fabled and trophy from the variant badges.
  - Not covered: a catch with a full inventory for everything else (the fish drops as an item entity), and the subtitle rarity check foecollab also does. Not seen in game yet.
- **Rarity** (`display/Rarity`): the item's custom_data `rarity` (`common`, `rare`, `epic`, `legendary`, `mythical`, `special`), fishing rods left out, colours from research section 2. Used by the rarity circles/squares (slots, hotbar, vault, equipment row). The square is 14x14 centred on the 16x16 item (2026-11-02, was the whole 18px slot, so neighbours touched), `rarityOpacity` (percent, default 40 = the old fixed 0x66, applies to circles and squares) and `rarityGradient` (square only, clear at the top and full at the bottom), both in the "menus" group of settings. Without foecollab the slot marks are drawn in `HandledScreenMixin.tacklebox$rarity`, with it in foecollab's slot markers (`foemixin/ItemMarkerHandlerMixin`).
- **Crew and friends** (hide nearby players) still come from foecollab (`compat/FoeFriends`); without it nobody is kept. Own lists need F8 dumps of the crew members and friends menus.
- Tests: `display/CatchWatchTest`, `display/CatchWatchIntegrationTest` (title, new fish, compendium count), `display/RarityTest` (square geometry and the 4 px gap). Not run in game yet.
