# Pet profit marks

- What it does (2026-10-16, asked for by luna; `display/PetSale`, setting `petProfitMarks`, off by default, "pet profit marks" in the menus page under "tooltips"): in the auction house, a pet that the pet store would pay more for than the listing costs gets a see-through green square behind its item, and every pet of a known rarity gets a line `sells for: $2.18K (+$176.38)` under the Price line (green with a plus when it is a profit, red with a minus when it is not). Display only: the item is never changed.
- The formula (worked out from luna's 12 `SELL »` pet store lines, exact to the cent, details in `fishonmc-research.md`): `payout = base × 2^((rating − 50) / 10)`, base 1250 for common and 2500 for rare. So 10 rating points double the payout and a rare is worth a common 10 points higher. The rating is the average of the four `percent_max` stats (climate luck and scale, location luck and scale), the same number the lore shows as the pet's rating. A common pet at a $2K price breaks even at about 56.8%.
- Where the price comes from: `renderInfo[0].money` in the listing's `custom_data` (exact float; the lore's `Price: $8.5K` is rounded). A pet without it (your own pets, other menus) gets nothing. The screen is not detected by its title: the item data decides, so the marks work on any page that lists pets with a price.
- Rarities: only `common` and `rare` have samples. Any other rarity (uncommon, epic, legendary) gets no tint and no line, on purpose: no guessing. Add a base to `PetSale.AT_FIFTY` once luna has `SELL »` lines for it, with the pet's rating.
- Hooks: the tint is an `@Inject` at the head of `HandledScreen.drawSlot` in `HandledScreenMixin` (fills 16×16 behind the item; `PetSale.of(ItemStack)` keeps one result per stack in a `WeakHashMap` because the item data is big and slots are asked every frame). The line is an `ItemTooltipCallback` in its own phase `pet_profit`, after the default one, in `Tacklebox.onInitializeClient`. `PetSale.addLine` copies the Price line's window prefix (the server's art glyphs) so the line sits inside the window, and does nothing when there is no Price line or window, or when the line is already there.
- Gotchas: the auction pages (title art `U+D541`–`U+D546`) keep the server's chest art, so the tint is drawn on that art. If luna has a sell boost, the bases above include it. If level changes the payout, the marks can only miss a profit on a leveled pet, not invent one, unless level lowers the payout (not seen).
- Tests: `PetSaleTest` (all pasted sell lines, the four dump pets: fox loses, bullfrog profits, duck loses, epic kangaroo left alone, break-even is not a profit, not-a-listing inputs, money format, the tooltip line and its colours, twice is the same, no Price line is left alone), `PetProfitIntegrationTest` (setting off for old saves and round trips, bullfrog item data to the finished line). Not run in game yet: the tint colour on the server's art, whether the added line fits the window art, and every rarity but common.
