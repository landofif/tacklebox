# Short tooltips (fish and pets)

- What it does (2026-10-16, asked for by luna; `display/FishTooltip` and `display/PetTooltip`, settings `shortFishTooltips` and `shortPetTooltips`, both off by default, "short fish tooltips" and "short pet tooltips" in the menus page under "tooltips"): fewer lines on a caught fish and on a pet. Display only: the item and its lore are never changed, only the list of lines the client draws, so foecollab (which reads `lore.get(15)` of a fish) is not affected.
  - **Fish**: loses the scientific name, the separator under it, the value line and the ecosystem line, shows weight and length as one line `0.17lb/7.11in` (no labels, no kg or cm) and moves the rarity mark onto the name line. A Camouflage Grouper goes from 20 lines to 14.
  - **Pet**: the rarity mark moves onto the name line, the kind line (`subtropical pet`) and the separator under it go, `Equipped Skin:` goes when it says Default or Empty, `Equipped Item:` and `Trail Item:` go when they say Empty, and the `right click to open pet menu` line goes. When any of those lines went, separators that ended up next to each other become one, and a separator with nothing but the end of the window after it goes. A Duck Pet goes from 31 lines to 18. Level, xp bar, climate and location bonuses, rating, Discoverer and Date stay.
- Hook: one `ItemTooltipCallback` in `Tacklebox.onInitializeClient` in its own phase ordered after the default one, where foecollab registers. By then foecollab has swapped the rarity mark to its `foecollab:tags` font; the mark's segments are moved as they are, so it keeps that font. A stack is a fish when its `custom_data` has the `fish` key and not `shopitem` (`FishTooltip.isFish`, so compendium species items, cod with lore and no custom_data, are left alone), a pet when `custom_data.type` is `pet` (`PetTooltip.isPet`). The pet profit line of the auction house (`pet-profit.md`) is added in its own phase next to the `Price:` line; neither feature touches the other's line, so their order does not matter.
- How it works: lines are found by label (`value:`, `weight:`, `equipped skin:`, matched on `Glyphs.plain`) or by shape (the mark line holds only spaces and glyphs, the separator is blank and struck through, the kind or scientific name is the unlabelled line under the mark), never by index. `PetTooltip` reuses the line helpers and the "move the mark" step of `FishTooltip`. F3+H lines after the window stay. Every line keeps the server's window prefix, so a removed line takes its slice of the window with it. Line 0 (the plain item name) is never touched. A rule whose line is not found does nothing, and a tooltip with no window prefix is left alone. Running it twice changes nothing more. The `/` between weight and length has the colour of the weight number.
- Layout of the lore: `fishonmc-research.md`, section 5 ("Fish tooltip layout" and "Pet tooltip layout").
- Gotchas: the vanilla name line (0) and the window's first line (1) both show the name; luna's screenshot shows it once, so which one the pack hides is not known, and the mark goes on line 1. Pets have one sample (a common Duck Pet): what the equipment lines say with something equipped is not known, so only the words Default and Empty are removed, and everything else stays. Bait, lures, rods and other items were not asked for and have no samples. Needs `/tacklebox debug item` (see `dev/build-and-debug.md`) to get a dump of anything new.
- Tests: `FishTooltipTest` (luna's real fish, each rule, mark font kept, variant glyph moves too, twice is the same, advanced lines stay, unknown layout left alone, `isFish`), `PetTooltipTest` (luna's real Duck Pet: 31 to 18, each rule, Empty skin goes, equipped lines stay with one separator around them, a price line and F3+H lines stay, separators untouched when nothing was removed, twice is the same, no window means untouched, `isPet`), `FishTooltipIntegrationTest` and `PetTooltipIntegrationTest` (old settings keep both off, each saved switch round-trips and the two are independent). Run in game by luna on 2026-10-16 (fish and pet, her words: "it works"). Not checked one by one: a legendary fish, pets of other rarities, a pet with something equipped, and a pet in the auction house.
