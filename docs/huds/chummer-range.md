# Chummer range ring (planned, not built)

- What luna wants (2026-10-16): find placed chummers in range and draw a circle for the range, built from scratch. FOECollab's chummer tracker looks and seems weird to her; her own foecollab config has its chummer hud and ring off (`showChummerHud=false`, `solidRangeBar=false`, `rangeRadius=20`).
- Range: 20 blocks horizontally, measured by luna from the centre (-1523.533 63 2018.497) to an edge (-1503.483 64 2018.643), 20.05 blocks. All new chummers have 20; some players still hold about year-old ones with 10 (the lore says `Range of 10 blocks`, `custom_data` has no range). Decision: assume 20.
- Item: `player_head`, `custom_data {bitespeed, rarity, timer:15.0f, type:"chummer"}`; bite speed by rarity 75, 130, 180, 250, 375. The server answers `Place the Chummer on a block.` when you use it off a block and refuses a second chummer nearby.
- **What a placed chummer is** (luna's dump 2026-10-17, `entities-20261017-011501.txt`, 2.5 blocks away): two display entities at the same block centre x and z (`-810.5`, `1065.5`). An `item_display` at y 27.48 (luna stood at 26.0, so about 1.5 above the ground) holding a `player_head` named `Chummer` with a fixed skin texture (url hash `7a257396...`, no `custom_data`), scale 1.2, billboard fixed. A `text_display` 0.5 higher (y 27.98, billboard vertical) whose lines are `Chummer™`, the rarity tag glyph (`` is common), `Activated by <name>`, a blank line, `Bite Speed: +75` and `14m 55s Remaining`, counted down by the server. So owner, bonus, rarity and the exact time left can all be read client-side from that text, and display entities are tracked far beyond render distance. Plenty of other `player_head` item displays exist nearby (named `textures's Head`, set decoration), so detect by the text display's `Chummer™` first line, not by the head. FOECollab never finds any of this: it takes the placer's name from the chat line, then the spot the placer is looking at (raycast 6 blocks, else their feet), so placers outside render distance are ignored. FOECollab draws a translucent wall 1 block tall (96 segments, `WorldRenderEvents.AFTER_ENTITIES`, `RenderLayers.debugQuads()`) and its own comments say the server draws a particle circle too.
- First step: `/tacklebox debug entities [radius]` (`DebugDump.entities`, default 24, max 64) dumps the crosshair target, every entity and every block entity around you to `config/tacklebox/guidump/entities-<time>.txt` and the clipboard: type, position, name, worn and held items with `custom_data` and skull profile, display entity contents, raw nbt. luna ran it next to a placed chummer on 2026-10-17 and it worked (150 entities, 11 block entities, no errors).
- Drawing (checked by compiling against the build.3 jar, nothing run): a flat ring from translucent quads in `WorldRenderEvents.AFTER_ENTITIES` (`net.fabricmc.fabric.api.client.rendering.v1.world`), camera position from `ctx.worldState().cameraRenderState.pos`, vertices camera-relative, `ctx.consumers().getBuffer(RenderLayers.debugQuads())` (cull off, depth write off, so lift the ring a little off the ground). Vanilla's `GizmoDrawing.circle` also exists but is a 20-gon. The mod has no world-space drawing yet.
