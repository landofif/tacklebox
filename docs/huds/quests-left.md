# Quests left hud

- Quests left (`hud/XpEstimate`, setting `questsHud`, `questsPos`, drawn in the `custom_huds` element, moved in foecollab's hud mover): quests until the next fishing level and the next level of the current location. Quest rewards are the middle of the `xp` and `lxp` ranges in the /quests tooltips (read while the menu is open); location xp per level is the wiki table `250*n^3` (20 levels); fishing xp per level is unpublished so it is learned from popup xp over xp bar movement (saved in `TackleboxSettings.need`, with the other numbers the hud reads, `questXp`, `profile`, `locLevels`); location level and progress come from `FishMenu.info` on location items in any open menu and advance with `LOCATION » x gained n xp` chat.
