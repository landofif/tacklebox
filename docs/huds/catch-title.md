# Catch title

- What (2026-11-14, luna: "similar to foecollab's", but more compact and customizable; `display/CatchTitle`, setting `catchTitle`, off by default): when you catch a fish, the fish's picture, under it one line with the rarity badge and the name, under that one line with the size class and `12.4ʟʙ / 31.7ɪɴ`. No box, text with a shadow. Display only, nothing is sent.
- **Picture**: the glyph the server sends as its own catch title (one char in `U+E000-E999`, research section 3), kept as the server's `Text` so its font and colour stay. A pack glyph can be any size and sit anywhere around the text line, so it is measured (`TextRenderer.prepare(..).getScreenRect()`, once per catch) and scaled to the height asked for: 32 pixels at `catchImageSize` 100. No shadow.
- **Line 1**: the server pack's rarity tag glyph (`U+F033`-`F037`, `F092`), with `catchVariant` on also the variant's tag glyph (`U+F041`-`F044`, `F098`, `F102`, `F179`) for a fish that is not normal, then the name in small capitals. Badges are drawn white with no shadow so they keep their own colours. luna picked the badge before the name over a coloured word, and the variant as a badge too.
- **Line 2**: the size word in the server's colour (research section 2), then weight and length with one decimal, `lb` and `in` only.
- **Where the data comes from**: the picture shows on the title, at once. The lines need the fish item: `CatchWatch.tick` finds the new fish of yours (`catches.md`) and hands its custom_data and item name to `CatchTitle.onFish` (name from the item name with pack glyphs taken out, else the species id; `rarity`, `variant`, `size`, `weight`, `length` from the custom_data). That is normally the next tick. With a full inventory the fish never gets there: 500 ms after the title, line 1 becomes the server's own subtitle as it was sent (it holds the rarity badge and the name) and there is no line 2.
- **The server's own title** is hidden while the catch title is on (`catchHideTitle`, on). `InGameHudOverlayMixin` does it at the TAIL of `setTitle` and `setSubtitle` by clearing `title`, `subtitle` and `titleRemainTicks`, not by cancelling at the head like the BITE! title: foecollab reads its catches from the same two methods and a cancel could run before its hooks. The subtitle counts as the catch's when it has a rarity badge and came within 1 s of the catch title, before or after it. Foecollab's own catch title is not touched; luna switches it off in foecollab's settings.
- **Settings** (settings > huds > "catch title", the tile has the on/off switch): hides the server's (`catchHideTitle`), picture and its size (`catchImage`, `catchImageSize` 25-300), rarity badge (`catchRarity`), variant badge (`catchVariant`, off), name (`catchName`), name in rarity colour (`catchNameRarity`, off), size class, weight, length (`catchSize`, `catchWeight`, `catchLength`), text colour (`catchColor`, the name and the numbers), shadow (`catchShadow`), seconds (`catchSeconds`, 3, 0.5-10), animation (`catchAnim`, the bite alert's list, `BobberTimer.anim` is shared), size (`catchScale`, 50-400).
- **Position**: `catchX` is the middle of the title in percent of the window width, `catchY` its top edge in percent of the height (50 / 25). The top is the anchor so the picture does not jump when the lines turn up a tick later. In the mover (`mover.md`) the box shows your last catch, or a made up legendary cod when there was none (its picture is `U+E000`, whatever the pack has there); drag moves, scroll sets `catchScale`, right click puts it back.
- Tests: `display/CatchTitleTest` (lines per switch, badges, number format, row layout, title and subtitle in both orders, fallback, end, the shared animation), `CatchTitleIntegrationTest` (settings tile switch, title, catch watch, lines; settings through save and load; the wiring), `screen/SettingsTilesIntegrationTest.hudTilesHaveSwitches`.
- **Not run in game yet.** To check: that the picture is the right size and sharp (the measured rectangle), that the rarity and variant badges show and line up with the name, what the item name of a fish really is (extra words or glyphs in it), what the subtitle looks like with a full inventory, that the server's title and subtitle are gone and nothing stale shows under a later title, that foecollab still counts the catch, and that foecollab's own catch title does not show on top.
