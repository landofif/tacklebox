# Power and compendium

- Power (2026-10-08, asked for by luna; `menu/Power`, `screen/PowerScreen`, `/tacklebox power`, "power" in the cloth tab "screens" group, settings `power`/`powerAt`/`variants`): `Power.scan` runs next to `XpEstimate.scan` on every open container screen (the original chest under `FishMenuScreen` too) and reads, with no clicks, the "Power Breakdown" item in /stats (any lore line with "total power"; the variant lines are told apart by their amount per fish because the names are pack glyphs) and the six variant lines `(caught/total)` of each compendium location item (all six must be there). Level power is exact: 50, 75 ... 14K for levels 1 to 20, the same at every location (luna confirmed), 64,000 per location, `Power.LEVEL_MAX` 1,088,000 for all 17. The screen is a semi solid panel like the bait picker: the total, one bar and four sources, the easiest next level (most power per xp left, with `XpEstimate.toNext` and `questLxp` for xp and quests) next to the location with the most fish left, the unique fish per variant, a "do this next" card (location, level to level, power, then the bait picker's best bait for the nearest rare-to-mythical collection and the variant to look for; power amounts end in the server pack glyph U+F064 everywhere), then the collections list (next level of every read collection, nearest first; collapsed to the nearest one until the "show all n" chip is clicked, not saved between openings), then a table of all locations sortable by next level, most power (level headroom plus fish) and fish left, with a tooltip per row; the whole page scrolls. Fish left is an upper bound (a species is counted once for power but once per location in the compendium). The collections overview page (`/collections`, 35 species collections) is read by `Power.overview`: "Current Level - [n]" and "(have/need)" give the next level n + 1, its power comes from the species table (50, 100, 250, 400, 1K, 5K, 10K, the same for all); a finished collection (its count reads `(30,039/MAX)`, `MenuTiles.maxed`) is dropped from the list; albino, all, fabled, the rarity collections, melanistic, trophy and gigantic are skipped there because their levels give other power (gigantic, at 8 of its 10 levels, was taken for a finished species collection until 2026-11-01, which erased what its level pages gave; `menu/PowerTest`) (they are read from their own level pages by `Power.collection`). Not run in game yet. Compendium completion and collections power are shown as read, their per location or per collection values are unknown (see research doc "power"). Not run in game yet. `XpEstimate.num` and `need` are public for it; `XpEstimate` lets Biwa pass level 20 (its saved progress equals the cap), `Power.row` clamps at 20.
