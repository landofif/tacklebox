# Fishonmc menu and presets

- fishonmc menu (`menu/FishMenu`, `menu/MenuRows`, `menu/Locations`, `screen/FishMenuScreen`, setting `fishMenu`, `menuSize`): `MinecraftClientMixin` swaps a `GenericContainerScreen` for `FishMenuScreen` when the player just typed `/travel`, `/turbotravel`, `/stats` or `/crew`. /menu (title `할`) and the instance list (`Instance 4 (14/75)` items; it shares the crew title `핒`, so the screen hands it back to the chest as soon as its items arrive, `FishMenu.isInstanceList`) stay the server's own chest (luna, 2026-10-30: "the default guis, just bigger"; `chestScale` 3 makes them bigger, see `menu-controls.md`). The collections overview and the location leveling pages share `핒` too: the panel names a `핒` menu by its items (`FishMenu.crewTitleMenu`: a "<x> Collection" item makes it "collections", "Location Leveling Data" makes it "location leveling", anything else is "crew"), so only the real crew menu says crew and lights the crew tab (2026-11-01; before, the collections page said "crew"). The daily missions column and the foecollab daily mission flag (`compat/FoeMainMenu`) went with the main menu panel. The button-only menus (stats, quests, crew, and anything opened from inside our screen, such as the quest difficulty and tier pages and compendium pages) use the same screen; menus that need your inventory (sell, vault, tackle shop, armor, auction house) stay as the chest. The screen reads the real chest slots, so it keeps working while the server refills them, and a click sends `ClickSlotC2SPacket` straight away with no client side pickup. Escape clicks the menu's Return button when it has one, otherwise closes; E closes.
- The rebuilt look (2026-10-30, asked for by luna after seeing someone's /event gui; it replaced the big-icon tiles, the rearrange mode and its `menuCells`, and the `menuAccent`/`menuAccentOpacity` tile fill, whose saved keys are ignored now): one solid dark panel (at most 600 wide, inside the `menuSize` frame; fully opaque since 2026-11-01, the hud showed through at 6% before; huds and chat beside it stay visible) with a 2 px accent gradient line on top (`Hypr.acc1/acc2`, luna's accent setting, not the orange of the screenshot). Header: the menu name in the accent, the fishing level, a bar and `1.18m/3.8m` from the profile head on the right (kept from your own last /stats page this session, so every tab shows it; empty until you open /stats). Tabs menu, travel, quests, stats, crew, event send that command (the same as typing it, not automation); the active one is underlined in the accent. "server menu" opens the chest (`FishMenu.bypass`), x closes. Footer hint bottom right (the stats page leaves out "wheel = scroll", it does not scroll).
- Rows (`MenuRows.of`): each server button is a 22 px row: its item (shulker, concrete and terracotta as flat colour swatches), its name in small caps, one dim line in sentence case, and on the right the `(a/b)` count from the lore with a thin bar (accent gradient, green when full; progress past the max, like the compendium's `11.85M/2.32M`, is full). The count sits on the name line and the bar on the second line, so the name only gives up the count's width and the line under it the bar's (2026-11-01; names were cut to "branwell's freshwater iii.." before). Numbers can end in k, m or b. The line is the first lore line that is not the name, not blank and not a small caps hint (`ᴄʟɪᴄᴋ ᴛᴏ ᴠɪᴇᴡ`; the server's small caps s is a plain s, so a hint is "no capital ascii letter and some small capital"); a quest joins `Catch 70x Rare fish` with `at Murray River`; a /travel place shows `Subtropical · Freshwater · -5°C`. Close and Return are not rows. The wheel scrolls the column under the mouse (the page wheel still turns pages first in paged menus). Hover shows the full item tooltip.
- Per menu: places, the collections overview and level pages are tiles (next point). Any other menu is one column of rows, two when they do not fit. In the quest menus the rows get the quest again marks (`QuestRedo`, see `quests.md`), and clicking a quest row tells `QuestRedo` which quest it was.
- Tile pages (2026-11-01, step 2 of the menu redesign, luna's picks from a mockup; `menu/MenuTiles` works out what a tile and its hover box say from names and lore, `FishMenuScreen.grid`/`drawGrid`/`drawTile`/`drawLevel` draw it). One grid, tiles at most 52 tall with 8 between them; they get shorter (down to 36) before the page scrolls (`MenuTiles.layout`; luna's 1920x1080 at gui scale 2 gives 580 by 462, so 35 collections fit at 52). The hovered tile gets a 1 px accent outline. Tile bars are one solid fill (a gradient is one fill per 4 px, on every tile) and round down, so 99.7% is not a full bar.
  - Collections overview (`핒` named "collections"): 5 columns, the item's icon, the short name (" Collection" dropped, two lines when long), the level big on the right, a bar. Order: closest to the next level first, ties in the server's order, finished ones last with a green "max" and a full green bar (`MenuTiles.byNextLevel`). Header right: "1 max · 7 almost there" (almost there is 90% or more). The hover box (not the item tooltip): the name, `Level 6, 43.1k/50k to level 7`, `Catch at cypress lake, lake biwa, ...`. Counts (`MenuTiles.count`) are written alike on both sides in the unit of what is needed: plain under 10,000 (`1,495/1,500`), k with one decimal, m and b with two; what you have rounds down, so 1,495/1.5K never reads "1.5k/1.5k". A collection is max when its count reads `(30,039/MAX)` (`MenuTiles.maxed`, luna's screenshot 2026-11-01), not by its level: species collections end at 7, gigantic, albino, fabled and the rarities at 10 (until 2026-11-01 level 7 counted as max, so gigantic at 8 showed max). The finished ones are read once when the page is built (`Grid.max`).
  - Places (4+ items named after a location: /travel and compendium location pages): 4 columns in the server's order (luna chose that over lowest level first, so the old "best place to fish" list is gone). No icon (all the same glowstone dust), the place's short name, the level big (from the tooltip via `FishMenu.info`, which only the compendium has, saved in `locationLevels` and reused in /travel; no number and no bar before you open the compendium once), a bar, level 20 or more is a green "max" (`MenuTiles.PLACE_TOP`, location leveling has 20 levels). A frenzy tile is green and a fabled one red with the word under the name; the header names them ("1 max · frenzy at toledo bend"). The hover box: name and mark, `Level 13, 28% to level 14`, the climate line, then the bait notes from `Locations` ("no bait noted for this place yet" without).
  - Level pages (5+ items named "Level n": a collection's level pages `학` and a place's location leveling): 5 columns, the number big, green when done (green concrete), accent for the one being worked on (yellow), faint later (anything else), the `have/need` count on the right for the ones not done, a bar. The header names the page from a level's lore ("albino collection" from its small caps line, "lake biwa" from `(Level 1) Lake Biwa`) and says "7/10 done". The other buttons (Location Leveling Data, Leveling Bonuses) are rows under the grid. Hover shows the server's tooltip, which has the rewards.
  - Not tiles: quests, crew, event, stats and other menus keep their rows. Not run in game yet.
- The /event page (2026-10-30, luna: "/event doesnt have a custom ui which i specifically asked for"; `hud/EventPage`, `FishMenuScreen.drawEvent`): `/event` and its title `함` (`Glyphs` names it "event") open in the panel, with an event tab. - The /stats page (2026-10-30, luna wanted it like SkyCrypt; `menu/StatsPage`, `FishMenuScreen.drawStats`): your own and `/stats <player>` use the same layout. `StatsPage.read` takes it as a stats page when it has both "Fishing Profile" and "General Statistics". Gear is found by custom_data `type` (hat, armor with `piece`, reel, pole, line, pet, bait or lure), the showcase by slots 2-6, the buttons by name (slot map in `fishonmc-research.md`). The server fills every free slot with a lore-less item named "Bread"; `StatsPage.filler` drops those (they took the button row and pushed the real buttons off the panel; the showcase's "Empty Profile Display" bread has lore and stays). Header: the profile's name instead of "stats", its level, bar and xp (the showcase trophy's placement, "#23 christmas event 2025", is gone since 2026-11-01: it is an old item, not a rank). Left: an `OtherClientPlayerEntity` wearing the chest, legs and boots (and a helmet if one shows up; `PresetsScreen.wearable`); the hat is a pack model on a leather boot, so it is only in the list. foecollab's `fun.hideArmor` would hide the set, so `compat/FoeArmor` switches it off while a stats page shows and puts it back when the screen closes (the presets screen does the same). The model is scaled by the tighter of the box's width and height (by the height only it was wider than the 130 wide box and the arms were cut), and its box is about as tall as the model. Another player's skin comes from the tab list when they are online, otherwise the default skin. Under it the five showcase slots, empty ones as faint boxes. Middle: armour (hat, helmet, chest, legs, boots) and rod (reel, pole, line with its durability bar), each row with rarity, quality and tier from the custom_data. Right: tiles power (profile), runes (Runes Breakdown), catches (General Statistics), bait stock (plain numbers of 1000 or more shortened like the servers power, 210.54k, 1.55m: `StatsPage.shorten`) (the bait; a lure without a `Stock:` line shows its name, at the normal size when it is too wide for big), laid out in order with no hole for a missing one, then the pet card with its level, equipped item and xp bar (at MAX it says "max" in green instead of a full bar). Bottom: the other buttons (fishing menu, collections, power breakdown, level colours, admiral colours) as small text buttons. Everything clicks its own slot and shows its tooltip. Only your own page fills the header level of the other tabs. Not run in game yet.

- Travel marks (2026-10-29, asked for by luna; `Locations.mark`/`isTravelItem`, `HandledScreenMixin.travelMark`, `FishMenuScreen.drawTile`): the server adds `*FRENZY!*` to the name of a place in a frenzy (dumps 2026-10-24 `Oil Rig *FRENZY!*`, 2026-10-29 `Toledo Bend Reservoir *FRENZY!*`, the first lore line too) and nothing else changes in the item. That place gets green (`FRENZY`), the fabled event's place red (`FABLED`, the place from the `Visit the X to` chat line, kept by `ServerHud.fabledPlace()` until `Caught by`; lost on a restart). Frenzy goes before fabled. The fabled event leaves no mark in the /travel items (2026-10-29 dump), so it can only come from chat. In the plain /travel chest the slot gets a see-through square behind the item (only items with `custom_data {fastTravel:1b}`, so no other menu); in tacklebox's menu panel (setting `fishMenu`) the whole tile, with "frenzy" or "fabled" under the name and in the header (since 2026-11-01; a row before). Always on, no setting. The check uses the full name string, not `Glyphs.plain`, in case the server puts the tag in a pack font. Root cause of "the frenzy tile is not green" (2026-10-29): luna's `fishMenu` was off, so /travel was the vanilla chest (the dump's screen is `class_476`) and the green was only drawn in tacklebox's screen. Witching Hour marks for Cypress Lake: luna said no. Tests: `TravelMarkTest`, `TravelMarksIntegrationTest`. Not seen in game yet.
