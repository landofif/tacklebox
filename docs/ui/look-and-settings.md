# Look, screens and settings

- **The look is foecollab's.** `ui/Foe` draws foecollab's `box_atlas` texture (`foecollab:textures/gui/sprites/elements/*.png`, a 15x15 atlas cut into 5px slices) by name, so the mod only looks right next to it. Colours: white text, grey `DIM`, green `TITLE` for panel titles, orange `ACCENT` for hovered and selected things (foecollab's own hover orange). `Foe.panel` is a box with its title inside the top edge; `Foe.button` is not a foecollab texture: a flat see-through dark fill, lighter grey on hover, no border (luna found the brown foecollab button "like poo" and said she does not like boxy buttons, 2026-10-06; `Foe.buttonOff` for disabled and picked). `Foe.text/bold/sentence` are the small caps and sentence case helpers. No custom fonts, no shadows to manage: use `drawTextWithShadow` like vanilla.
- Screens extend `ui/TackleboxScreen`: the normal game background with no frame (luna had the box at its faintest and asked for the outline gone, 2026-10-06), its title in the top left corner (don't draw the title yourself; content starts around y 23). Override `framed()` to false for screens that must show the game. Widgets are vanilla (`ButtonWidget`, `TextFieldWidget`, `SliderWidget`), except that `PressableWidgetMixin` (setting `flatButtons`, asked for by luna 2026-10-06) draws every button, the game's own too, as a `Foe.button` (`Foe.buttonOff` when disabled) instead of the bevelled texture; only `screen/RowListWidget` is ours, a scroll list without the textured strip.
- **Settings are the mods own screen again** (2026-10-09, asked for by luna: separate from foecollab's, hyprland look and animation). `screen/SettingsScreen` (`/tacklebox`, the open key, Mod Menu configure) with `ui/Hypr` (rounded rects, gradient rings, hyprland's bezier curves, catppuccin style palette) is the one place the box-free rules do not apply: a waybar style bar (a pill per page, the active pill glides, scroll or alt+number switches, search pill, red close dot), the sections of a page as rounded tiles in a masonry grid; the tile under the mouse is focused and gets the rotating cyan to green border. Tiles pop in staggered (overshoot curve), a page change slides and fades, closing pops out (`close()` only starts it, `tick()` leaves). `screen/SettingsPages` holds the rows (toggle, slider, cycle, colour, action), moved from the old cloth `ClothTab`; values apply live and save in `removed()`. The cloth tab in foecollab's config, `ConfigScreenProviderMixin`, `ButtonEntry` and `FoeScreens.config` are gone (foecollab's config is untouched now). Colours open `ColorPickerScreen`, the viewmodel settings stay their own screen (a link). Not run in game yet. The accent gradient is configurable (`accent1`/`accent2`, default cyan 33ccff to mint 00ff99, "accent" tile on the screens page, `Hypr.acc1()/acc2()`), and the shared pieces carry the look into every mod screen: `Foe.button` is a rounded fill with a gradient outline on hover, `TextFieldWidgetMixin` rounds text fields (both under `flatButtons`), `Foe.title` and list scrollbars use the accent. Screens with their own panels (power, bait picker, fish menu) keep their palettes; `TriggerListScreen` is the model for a full restyle.
- **Click to open tiles (2026-11-01, asked for by luna: "not show all the settings at once").** Every page shows its tiles small in a grid (`SettingsNav.grid`, 4/3/2/1 columns by width, 40 high): name, "n settings", and the tile's on/off switch at the right if it has one. Clicking the switch flips it (hover shows its help); clicking anywhere else opens the tile alone in the middle (420 wide) with all its rows, "‹ page" in its title row and "page › tile" in the bar. Esc, the title row or a page change go back to the grid; esc on the grid closes. Search still shows the matching rows open from every page and drops an opened tile. The switch is marked by hand with `main()` after the toggle in `SettingsPages` (a hud's own on/off, presets screen, vault overview, ask before disconnect, custom line colour, hide far displays, chummer ring, screenshot preview); tiles like hotbar, menus or chat have none. `SettingsNav` holds page and opened tile. Tests: `SettingsNavTest`, `SettingsTilesIntegrationTest`. Not run in game yet.
- Hotbar and equipment (2026-10-09, asked for by luna): `BoxLook.HYPR`/`SelectionStyle.HYPR` are the look you pick (since 2026-10-20 new installs start at `BoxLook.DEFAULT` = vanilla; saved settings moved once by `lookVersion`), the hotbar box is a rounded pill filled with the accent gradient at `boxOpacity` and the selection is `display/HotbarSelection`, a ring that glides between slots. `hud/EquipmentRow` is the mods own armour and rod parts row (`equipHud`; rod parts from the rod's custom_data `reel/pole/line`, decoded with `ItemStack.OPTIONAL_CODEC`, the log says "could not read the rod's" if that guess is wrong); `EquipmentHudMixin` only switches foecollab's row off. `PresetsScreen` cards are hyprland tiles. Not run in game yet.
- **Huds and inventories have no box, no outline.** luna wants them transparent (2026-10-06, "we dont need this slop"; she had both opacity sliders at their minimum): the quests left hud and the chat boxes are text with a shadow, the title in green (`Foe.title`), and `HandledScreenMixin` draws chests, shulkers, hoppers, droppers and the inventory as just the items, the title where a box would put it and the player preview with no frame (the vanilla hover highlight stays). `hudOpacity` is gone. Do not put a `Foe.box` or `Foe.panel` behind a hud or an inventory; the screens have none either (`screenOpacity` is gone); `Foe.box`/`Foe.panel` are left for the little panels inside a screen and `Foe.button` for buttons and tiles. `flatMenus` = "simple menus". fishonmc menu art is glyphs in the title, so `util/Glyphs.menuName` strips private-use and hangul glyphs and pack-font runs, and names art-only titles from research section 8. Optional background behind them (2026-10-08, asked for by luna; `display/MenuBackground`, settings `menuBgColor/menuBgOpacity/menuImageOpacity`, picture `config/tacklebox/menu.png` cropped to cover, both default 0 = invisible, in the "menus" group of the cloth tab). Not run in game yet. Furnaces, anvils and so on keep their textures, because the progress arrows and buttons are in them.

- **Opt in (2026-10-20, asked for by luna):** every feature toggle defaults to off on new installs (menus, fishonmc menu, presets, vaults, huds, level bars, now playing, server hud, bobber tweaks, chat hiding, chummer ring). The rows of a feature (level bar rows, now playing rows) stay on so switching the feature on shows something. A saved settings.json keeps its own values.
